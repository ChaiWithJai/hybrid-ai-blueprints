# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (SPA)
## PROJECT AEROFLUX: €850,000,000 STRATEGIC ACQUISITION OF AERODYNAMICS SA

**Buyer:** Vortex Global Aerospace Corp. (Delaware, USA)  
**Seller:** AeroDynamics SA Shareholder Syndicate (Toulouse, France)  
**Purchase Price:** €850,000,000 EUR Cash & Stock Consideration  
**Governing Law:** French Commercial Code & English Common Law Jurisdiction  

---

### ARTICLE I: PURCHASE PRICE & WORKING CAPITAL ADJUSTMENTS

#### Section 1.01: Aggregate Consideration Breakdown
* **Cash Consideration:** €600,000,000 EUR funded via Buyer's corporate revolving facility and cash-on-hand.
* **Stock Consideration:** €200,000,000 EUR equivalent in newly issued Vortex Global Common Shares (NASDAQ: VRTX).
* **Indemnity Escrow Amount:** €50,000,000 EUR (held for 18 months in escrow at BNP Paribas).
* **Target Normalized Net Working Capital:** €45,000,000 EUR (subject to euro-for-euro dollar adjustment at closing).

---

### ARTICLE II: REGULATORY CONDITIONS PRECEDENT & CFIUS COVENANTS

#### Section 2.01: Mandatory Regulatory Approvals
Closing is subject to the following non-waivable regulatory conditions:
1. **European Commission Antitrust:** Approval under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 without Phase II divestiture requirements exceeding €25M in annual revenue.
2. **CFIUS Clearance:** Joint voluntary notice filed with the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States regarding military drone navigation IP.
3. **French Foreign Direct Investment (FDI):** Written authorization from the French Ministry of the Economy (Direction Générale du Trésor) under Article L. 151-3 of the French Monetary and Financial Code.

---

### ARTICLE III: SYNERGY REALIZATION & MATERIAL ADVERSE EFFECT (MAE)

#### Section 3.01: Material Adverse Effect Definition
An MAE shall exclude changes in general economic conditions, aerospace industry-wide supply chain disruptions, or foreign exchange rate fluctuations between the Euro (€) and US Dollar ($), provided such changes do not disproportionately affect AeroDynamics SA compared to peer European aerospace manufacturers.
