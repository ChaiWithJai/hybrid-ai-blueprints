**Dated as of:** February 1, 2026

### ARTICLE II: FINANCIAL COVENANTS & LEVERAGE STEP-DOWNS

#### Section 2.01: Maximum First Lien Net Leverage Ratio
The Borrower covenants that the First Lien Net Leverage Ratio as of the end of each Fiscal Quarter shall not exceed the levels set forth below:

| Period | First Lien Leverage Cap | Total Net Leverage Cap | Minimum Interest Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Closing through Q4 2026 | **4.75 : 1.00** | **6.50 : 1.00** | **2.75 : 1.00** |
| Q1 2027 through Q4 2027 | **4.25 : 1.00** | **5.75 : 1.00** | **3.00 : 1.00** |
| Q1 2028 through Q4 2028 | **3.75 : 1.00** | **5.00 : 1.00** | **3.25 : 1.00** |
| Q1 2029 and thereafter | **3.25 : 1.00** | **4.25 : 1.00** | **3.50 : 1.00** |

#### Section 2.02: Mandatory Excess Cash Flow (ECF) Prepayment Sweep
Within 90 days following each fiscal year end, the Borrower shall prepay outstanding Term Loan B principal in an amount equal to the applicable ECF percentage:
* **50.0% of ECF** if First Lien Net Leverage Ratio $> 4.00\times$.
* **25.0% of ECF** if First Lien Net Leverage Ratio is between $3.25\times$ and $4.00\times$.
* **0.0% of ECF** if First Lien Net Leverage Ratio $< 3.25\times$.

### ARTICLE III: RESTRICTED PAYMENTS & DIVIDEND BASKET

#### Section 3.01: Builder Basket Conditions
Dividends to Sponsor Equity Holders (Restricted Payments) are strictly prohibited unless all of the following conditions are met:
1. No Event of Default has occurred and is continuing.
2. Total Net Leverage Ratio is pro-forma $\le 4.25\times$ (or First Lien $\le 3.00\times$).
3. Aggregate dividend amount does not exceed the available "Builder Basket" ($50\%$ of cumulative Consolidated Net Income since Closing Date plus equity contributions).
