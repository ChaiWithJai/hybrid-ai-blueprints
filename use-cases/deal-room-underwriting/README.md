# Deal room underwriting

A private equity deal professional needs a fast first review of an authorized
M&A folder. The review must identify the transaction, financing, material
risks, missing information, and the next decision.

## Job

Given an authorized M&A folder and an investment screen, prepare a source linked
first pass underwriting brief. The brief must help a deal professional decide
whether to advance, pause, or stop review and state the next action.

The job begins when the system accepts the folder snapshot and investment
screen. The job ends when the brief, citations, calculations, trace, and review
record are available at stable URLs.

## Primary user

The primary user is an associate or vice president who owns the first review.
A partner, operating executive, or technology leader may be the economic buyer.
A qualified deal professional must own the expected answer and the final domain
review.

## Selected tasks

The first benchmark covers:

1. [Transaction chronology](tasks/transaction-chronology.yaml)
2. [Financing analysis](tasks/financing-analysis.yaml)
3. [Regulatory analysis](tasks/regulatory-analysis.yaml)

The three tasks test different source forms and different failure risks. They
also produce work that a reviewer can inspect against public records.

## Valuable result

The valuable result is an expert accepted first pass brief that lowers review
time without hiding uncertainty or increasing decision risk. The business
study records time, corrections, evidence quality, usefulness, workflow
preference, and willingness to pay after the user has completed the workflow.

[Read the economic case](economic-case.md).

## Limits

The use case does not define a completed investment committee memo. Many deal
rooms lack the market work, management evidence, and judgment needed for a full
memo. A human remains responsible for the investment decision.
