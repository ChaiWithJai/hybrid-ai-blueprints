# Economic case for deal room underwriting

The economic case starts with a unit of work that a buyer understands. The
unit is one expert accepted first pass review for one deal room.

## Source of the hypothesis

The Agents' Last Exam economic frontier study ranked 153 published professional
tasks. It estimated value from loaded labor cost, human time, annual frequency,
risk, review cost, market gap, deployment readiness, and an assumed Bonsai
success rate.

The study found repeated opportunities in document work that needs structured
outputs, citations, schemas, and review routing. Deal room underwriting has the
same operating needs, so the study supports testing the use case.

The study does not prove demand or product quality. Its workload values and
success rates are assumptions. Customer observations must replace them before
PrismML makes an investment or pricing decision.

[Read the study record](../../research/agents-last-exam-economic-frontier/README.md).

## Value model

The first business model uses:

```text
annual value = annual task frequency
             x human hours per task
             x loaded hourly cost
             x accepted task success rate
             x risk adjustment
             - review cost
             - compute cost
             - deployment cost
```

Every input must state whether it is an assumption or an observation. A
blueprint evaluation supplies task success, review time, and correction burden.
A customer study supplies frequency, labor cost, willingness to pay, and the
value of faster decisions.

## Pricing study

The pricing study follows the product and pricing method associated with
Madhavan Ramanujam. The team tests the product and price together. The buyer
uses the workflow before answering price questions.

The study records:

- The acceptable price
- The price that feels expensive
- The price that feels too expensive
- The preferred value unit
- The preferred package
- A paid next step or a reason to decline

No price is approved until a qualified buyer completes the workflow and signs
the resulting record.
