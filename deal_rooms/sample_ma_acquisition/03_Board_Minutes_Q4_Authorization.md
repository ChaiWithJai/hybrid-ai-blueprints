### ITEM 1: PROPOSED ACQUISITION OF NOVATECH DYNAMICS INC.
The CEO presented the definitive terms for the acquisition of **NovaTech Dynamics Inc.** for an aggregate purchase price of **$140,000,000 USD**, structured as:
* $95,000,000 USD in cash consideration funded via new senior secured credit draw.
* $35,000,000 USD in preferred equity rollover issued to NovaTech founders.
* $10,000,000 USD in contingent milestone earnouts.

**Lender Consent Analysis:**  
CFO Elena Rostova noted that under Section 3.01 of the Meridian Credit Agreement, acquisitions over $100M require 66.67% lender approval and a pro-forma Leverage Ratio $\le 3.25\times$. The pro-forma leverage ratio is estimated at $3.42\times$, meaning a formal covenant waiver or equity injection of $15,000,000 is required prior to closing.

**Resolution 2025-12-B:**  
The Board unanimously voted to approve the execution of the Merger Agreement subject to obtaining Required Lender consent and covenant waiver from Meridian Global Capital Bank.

### ITEM 2: DISCLOSURE OF PENDING INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY LITIGATION
General Counsel Jonathan Zhao briefed the Board regarding a newly filed patent infringement complaint by *Quantum Sensor Systems LLC* in the District of Delaware (Case No. 25-cv-09822).
* **Allegation**: Infringement of US Patent No. 9,842,110 covering automated industrial lidar telemetry.
* **Plaintiff's Claimed Damages**: **$12,500,000 USD** in treble damages plus ongoing royalty demands.
* **Credit Agreement Compliance**: Under Section 4.02, this litigation exceeds the $7,500,000 threshold and must be formally disclosed to Meridian Global Capital Bank within five (5) business days.

**Resolution 2025-12-C:**  
The Board directed external counsel (Kirkland & Ellis LLP) to enter appearance and instructed CFO Rostova to submit formal written disclosure to Administrative Agent Meridian.
