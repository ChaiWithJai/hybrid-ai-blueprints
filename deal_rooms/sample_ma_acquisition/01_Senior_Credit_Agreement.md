# SENIOR SECURED CREDIT AGREEMENT

**Dated as of November 14, 2024**  
**Between:** APEX INDUSTRIAL HOLDINGS LLC (as "Borrower")  
**And:** MERIDIAN GLOBAL CAPITAL BANK (as "Administrative Agent" and "Collateral Agent")  
**Total Facility Amount:** $150,000,000 USD  

---

## ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS & ACCOUNTING TERMS

### Section 1.01: Defined Terms
* **"Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA"**: For any trailing twelve-month (TTM) period, Consolidated Net Income plus (without duplication) Interest Expense, Tax Expense, Depreciation, Amortization, minus non-recurring extraordinary gains, and permitted add-backs not to exceed 15.0% of unadjusted EBITDA.
* **"Consolidated Total Net Debt"**: Total outstanding funded debt minus unencumbered cash and cash equivalents up to a maximum credit of $10,000,000.
* **"Consolidated Leverage Ratio"**: The ratio of Consolidated Total Net Debt to Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA as of the last day of any fiscal quarter.
* **"Interest Coverage Ratio"**: The ratio of Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA to Consolidated Cash Interest Expense for the trailing twelve-month period.
* **"Minimum Liquidity Threshold"**: Unrestricted cash and available borrowing capacity under the revolving facility of not less than $20,000,000 at all times.

---

## ARTICLE II: FINANCIAL COVENANTS

### Section 2.01: Maximum Consolidated Leverage Ratio
The Borrower shall not permit the Consolidated Leverage Ratio, calculated as of the last day of each fiscal quarter set forth below, to exceed the corresponding ratio:

| Fiscal Quarter Period | Maximum Permitted Leverage Ratio | Step-Down Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Q1 2025 through Q4 2025 | **3.75 : 1.00** | Initial Covenant Level |
| Q1 2026 through Q2 2026 | **3.50 : 1.00** | First Scheduled Step-Down |
| Q3 2026 and thereafter | **3.25 : 1.00** | Final Step-Down |

### Section 2.02: Minimum Interest Coverage Ratio
The Borrower shall maintain an Interest Coverage Ratio of not less than **4.00 : 1.00** for each trailing twelve-month period ending on the last day of each fiscal quarter.

### Section 2.03: Minimum Liquidity Requirement
The Borrower shall maintain unrestricted cash plus undrawn revolving credit commitments of at least **$20,000,000 USD** as of the close of business on the last business day of each calendar month.

---

## ARTICLE III: NEGATIVE COVENANTS & ACQUISITION RESTRICTIONS

### Section 3.01: Permitted Acquisitions & Capital Expenditures
The Borrower shall not, without the prior written consent of the Required Lenders (holding at least 66.67% of aggregate commitments):
1. Consummate any single acquisition exceeding **$100,000,000 USD** in total enterprise value without delivering a pro-forma compliance certificate demonstrating a pro-forma Leverage Ratio $\le 3.25\times$.
2. Incur annual non-financed Capital Expenditures in excess of **$18,000,000 USD** for fiscal year 2025 or **$22,000,000 USD** for fiscal year 2026.

### Section 3.02: Change of Control Mandatory Prepayment
Upon the occurrence of a Change of Control (defined as any person or group acquiring $\ge 35.0\%$ of voting equity, or the replacement of a majority of the Board of Directors), the Borrower shall prepay all outstanding loans at 101% of principal plus accrued interest within 30 calendar days.

---

## ARTICLE IV: EVENTS OF DEFAULT & CROSS-DEFAULT

### Section 4.01: Cross-Default Provisions
An Event of Default shall occur if the Borrower or any Subsidiary fails to pay any principal or interest on any Material Indebtedness exceeding **$5,000,000 USD** in the aggregate when due, or if any default occurs permitting the acceleration of such Material Indebtedness.

### Section 4.02: Material Litigation Disclosures
The Borrower must disclose any pending litigation, arbitration, or regulatory proceeding with potential liabilities exceeding **$7,500,000 USD** within five (5) business days of notice.
